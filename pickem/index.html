How it works

  • Every Big 12 game, every week. Conference games and non-conference games alike — if a Big 12 team is playing, it's on the sheet.
  • Straight up, no spreads. Just pick who wins.
  • Every game is worth exactly 1 point. A blowout between two teams nobody cares about counts the same as the Holy War.
  • Everything locks at the first kickoff of the week. One deadline for the whole slate — usually Thursday night. Miss it and those games are zeroes, so get them in early.
  • Nobody sees your picks until the lock. After that, everyone's picks are public for the week.
  • Miss a game, take the zero. No make-ups.
  • Most correct picks at the end of the season wins. That's the whole thing.
