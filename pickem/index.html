How it works

  • Every Big 12 game, every week. If a Big 12 team is playing, it's on the sheet.
  • Straight up. No spreads to cover — just pick who wins.
  • Underdogs pay more. A favorite is worth 1 point. Call an underdog correctly and it's worth 2 (+3 to +9.5), 3 (+10 to +17.5), or 4 (+18 or more). Each card shows what it pays before you pick. A wrong pick is always 0 — you can never lose points for swinging big.
  • The lines are set Sunday at 6am and never move. That's when picks open. Whatever a game pays when you first see it is what it pays all week, so there's no advantage to waiting for a better number. Games without a posted line are worth 1.
  • You have from Sunday 6am to the first kickoff. Picks open at the same time every week and close when the week's earliest game starts — usually Thursday evening. Miss it and those games are zeroes.
  • Lock in early if you want to see everyone else. Once all your games are picked you can lock in before the deadline. Your picks go final immediately, and in exchange you see the picks of everyone else who has locked in — and only them. People still deciding stay hidden.
  • If you never lock in, you're locked in automatically at the deadline, and then everybody's picks are public.
  • Most points at the end of the season wins. That's the whole thing.
