How it works

  • Every Big 12 game, every week. If a Big 12 team is playing, it's on the sheet.
  • Straight up. No spreads — just pick who wins.
  • Every game is worth 1 point. A blowout counts the same as the Holy War.
  • Everything locks at the first kickoff of the week. One deadline for the whole slate, usually Thursday night. Get them in early.
  • Nobody sees your picks until the lock. After that the whole week is public.
  • Miss a game, take the zero. No make-ups.
  • Most correct picks at the end of the season wins. That's the whole thing.
